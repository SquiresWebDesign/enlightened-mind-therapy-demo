Meet the founder

Nathan
Russell.

LMSW · QMHP · QIDP

Hi, I'm Nathan — a licensed therapist with a passion for helping people find calm, clarity, and connection in their lives.

I earned my Master's in Social Work from Wayne State University and have spent years working with individuals facing trauma, PTSD, and substance use challenges.

I have spent my professional career working at the Department of Veterans Affairs, Community Mental Health, and substance use treatment facilities.

My goal is simple: to create a space where you feel understood, supported, and empowered to heal at your own pace.

Our Approach

Meet yourself
where you are.

Each person's path to healing is unique. Therapy is designed to meet you where you are and help you move forward safely and effectively.

01

Person-Centered

A compassionate environment focused on your experiences, goals, strengths, and individual needs.

02

Evidence-Based

Therapeutic approaches are thoughtfully selected to support meaningful progress and lasting growth.

03

Whole-Person

Recognizing the relationship between thoughts, emotions, behaviors, the body, and connection with others.

Services

Support for
your next chapter.

Enlightened Mind Therapy offers therapeutic approaches designed around the individual and the challenges they are navigating.

01

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

Helps you recognize and reshape unhelpful thought patterns that affect emotions and actions.

↗
02

Trauma-Focused Therapy

Uses a body-centered, bottom-up approach to help regulate the nervous system, release stored stress, and rebuild emotional safety.

↗
03

Prolonged Exposure Therapy

Helps clients safely confront difficult emotions and memories through a structured therapeutic approach.

↗
04

Substance Use & Recovery Support

Focuses on reconnecting with yourself and others while building strength through community and understanding.

↗
05

Acceptance & Commitment Therapy

Encourages acceptance of difficult thoughts and feelings while creating room for values and purposeful action.

↗
06

Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy

Combines cognitive techniques with mindfulness to support awareness and emotional regulation.

↗
07

EMDR

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing is designed to help process distressing memories in a safe, guided environment.

↗
08

Substance Use Disorder Groups

A supportive space to understand triggers, build healthier habits, and work toward recovery.

↗
