01 WELCOME

A safe space to heal, reflect, and grow.

Life brings change, loss, and moments that challenge our sense of balance. At Enlightened Mind Therapy, healing begins with understanding — helping you connect thoughts, emotions, and behaviors in a way that restores inner calm and resilience.

Whether you are working through trauma, navigating recovery, or learning to manage anxiety, the approach combines evidence-based therapies with compassionate, person-centered care.

02 OUR APPROACH

Your path is your own.

Each person's path to healing is unique. Therapy is designed to meet you where you are and help you move forward safely and effectively.

05 OUR PHILOSOPHY

Healing is not a destination.

At Enlightened Mind Therapy, healing is viewed as a process of becoming present with yourself. The goal is to create an environment where empathy meets science — integrating techniques that honor both the body and mind.

More than simply treating symptoms, the work is centered around rediscovering balance, purpose, and connection.

06 THE FOUNDER

Nathan Russell.

LMSW, QMHP, QIDP

Founder of Enlightened Mind Therapy

Hi, I'm Nathan — a licensed therapist with a passion for helping people find calm, clarity, and connection in their lives.

I earned my Master’s in Social Work from Wayne State University and have spent years working with individuals facing trauma, PTSD, and substance use challenges.

I have spent my professional career working at the Department of Veterans Affairs, Community Mental Health, and substance use treatment facilities.

My goal is simple: to create a space where you feel understood, supported, and empowered to heal at your own pace.
