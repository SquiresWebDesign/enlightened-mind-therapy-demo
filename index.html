A thoughtful approach

A safe space to heal, reflect, and grow.

Life brings change, loss, and moments that can challenge our sense of balance. Therapy can provide a place to slow down, understand what you are experiencing, and develop practical tools for moving forward.

Enlightened Mind Therapy brings together evidence-based approaches with compassionate, person-centered care. The goal is to help you better understand the connection between thoughts, emotions, behaviors, and the body.

Meet the founder

Nathan Russell LMSW, QMHP, QIDP

Founder of Enlightened Mind Therapy

Hi, I'm Nathan — a licensed therapist with a passion for helping people find calm, clarity, and connection in their lives.

Nathan earned his Master’s in Social Work from Wayne State University and has worked with individuals facing trauma, PTSD, and substance use challenges.

His professional experience includes work with the Department of Veterans Affairs, Community Mental Health, and substance use treatment facilities.

The goal is simple: to create a space where people feel understood, supported, and empowered to heal at their own pace.

Our philosophy

Healing is a process.

At Enlightened Mind Therapy, healing is viewed as a process of becoming more present with yourself.

Therapy can bring together empathy and science while honoring both the mind and body.

Rather than simply focusing on symptoms, the work can include discovering balance, purpose, connection, and practical skills that support everyday life.

“Meet yourself where you are, then begin moving forward.”
