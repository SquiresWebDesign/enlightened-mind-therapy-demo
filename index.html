Meet the founder

Nathan
Russell.

LMSW • QMHP • QIDP

Nathan Russell is the founder of Enlightened Mind Therapy. He earned his Master’s in Social Work from Wayne State University and has professional experience working with individuals facing trauma, PTSD, and substance use challenges.

His professional background includes work with the Department of Veterans Affairs, Community Mental Health, and substance use treatment facilities.

His goal is to create a space where people feel understood, supported, and empowered to move forward at their own pace.
