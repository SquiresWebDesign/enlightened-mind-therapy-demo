04 OUR PHILOSOPHY

Healing is a process.

Enlightened Mind Therapy approaches healing as an ongoing process of becoming more present with yourself.

The goal is to bring empathy and evidence-informed care together while respecting the connection between the mind, body, relationships, and everyday life.

Rather than focusing only on symptoms, therapy can help you rediscover balance, purpose, and connection.

06 ABOUT NATHAN

A therapist who listens.

Nathan Russell, LMSW, QMHP, QIDP

Nathan is a licensed therapist and the founder of Enlightened Mind Therapy. His work is centered around helping people feel understood, supported, and empowered throughout their healing process.

He earned his Master of Social Work from Wayne State University and has professional experience with veterans, community mental health, trauma, PTSD, substance-use treatment, and recovery services.

His goal is to provide a comfortable environment where clients can work at their own pace while developing the tools and understanding needed to move forward.
